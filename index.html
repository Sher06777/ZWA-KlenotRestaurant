Agave restaurant: Mexiko v srdci Prahy

Fine dining restaurant in the heart of Prague

Uncover hidden secrets of Prague´s Lesser town and explore culinary art inspired by Augustinian monks. Augustine restaurant located in 13th century monastery will definitely delight you, not only with its rich history and calm atmosphere, but also with top service and culinary experience created by mastermind chef Jan Horak and his team.

Each dish perfectly blends flavors and textures into a truly culinary adventure. From classics to unexpected delights, our menu is full of treasures. Fresh ingredients and presentation make every bite memorable. Experience premium taste and attention in our kitchen.
