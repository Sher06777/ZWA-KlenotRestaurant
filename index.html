About Klenot Restaurant

Located in the heart of Prague, Klenot Restaurant is a place where tradition meets elegance. We specialize in authentic Czech and European cuisine, prepared with fresh local ingredients and a touch of modern presentation.

Our philosophy is simple: quality, hospitality, and unforgettable atmosphere. Whether you're visiting for a romantic dinner, a business meeting, or a family celebration, we aim to provide an experience that goes beyond just food.

Our chefs combine classic recipes with contemporary techniques, offering seasonal dishes, signature specialties, and a curated wine selection. At Klenot Restaurant, every detail — from the interior design to the plating — is created to make you feel special.
